The Mediran Chronicles

The Bastion

The V.E.R.U. agents’ stronghold and the people who keep it

How the Bastion Works

Every 7 days the Bastion takes a Bastion turn. On each turn, the agents can give one order to each special facility, and that facility’s keepers carry it out while the agents are away. Most orders take the full 7 days, and a facility can’t take a new order until the last one is finished.

Instead of giving orders, the agents can tell the whole Bastion to Maintain: everyone tends to upkeep, no other orders are given that turn, and the DM rolls once for a Bastion event. If no agent is at the Bastion or able to reach its keepers, by Sending or similar magic, the Bastion maintains itself.

Facilities
Barrack
Recruit
Special Facility · Roomy
Kept by Tomoe Dagaki
Bunks, a weapon rack, and a drill yard for the people who hold the Bastion when the agents are away.
Quarters. The Barrack houses the Bastion’s defenders and can hold up to twelve Bastion Defenders.
Recruit: Bastion Defenders. The hireling recruits up to four Bastion Defenders at no cost, and they move into the Barrack. This order can’t be issued while the Barrack is full.
Enlarging the Barrack. For 2,000 GP, the Barrack can be enlarged to a Vast facility that holds up to twenty-five Bastion Defenders.
Greenhouse
Harvest
Special Facility · Roomy
Kept by Ambrose Bullock
Glass walls, warm air, and plants growing well out of their season.
Fruit of Restoration. One plant in the Greenhouse is magical and bears three fruits at a time. A creature that eats one of these fruits gains the benefit of a Lesser Restoration spell. A fruit loses its magic if it isn’t eaten within 24 hours of being picked. The plant regrows its picked fruit each day at dawn, and it dies if anyone tries to move it out of the Greenhouse.
Harvest: Healing Herbs. Over 7 days and at no cost, the hireling grows and prepares one Potion of Healing (greater).
Harvest: Poison. Over 7 days and at no cost, the hireling extracts one application of Assassin’s Blood, Malice, Pale Tincture, or Truth Serum, kept in a vial.
Teleportation Circle
Recruit
Special Facility · Roomy
Permanent Circle. A permanent teleportation circle, as created by the Teleportation Circle spell, is inscribed on the floor of this facility.
Recruit: Spellcaster. An invitation is sent out through the circle; roll any die. On an odd number, the invitation is declined and the order has no effect. On an even number, a Friendly NPC spellcaster arrives through the circle and stays for 14 days or until it casts a spell for the agents.
While the Spellcaster Stays. You can ask the spellcaster to cast one Wizard spell of level 4 or lower, or level 8 or lower once you reach level 17. You pay for any costly Material components the spell requires. The spellcaster won’t defend the Bastion and leaves at once if the Bastion is attacked.
Trophy Room
Research
Special Facility · Roomy
Kept by Meredith Inkwell and Tomás Goldleaf
Keepsakes from the agents’ assignments, labeled and shelved by people who know exactly where everything came from.
Research: Lore. Over 7 days, the hirelings study a legend, a type of creature, or a famous object of your choice, and learn up to three accurate facts about it that you didn’t already know. The DM decides what they learn.
Research: Trinket Trophy. Over 7 days, the hirelings search for a magic item; roll any die. On an odd number, they find nothing. On an even number, they find a magic item, rolled on the Implements—Common table.
