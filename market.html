The Mediran Chronicles

The Market

Medir trades on the Svirask standard, set centuries ago by the bankers of House Svirask in Solrest: ten copper pieces to the silver, ten silver to the gold, and ten gold to the platinum.

These are the usual prices of established merchants in a major city. Remote towns charge more for what they have to haul in, and the great trade hubs often charge less. Guild-affiliated merchants who value a long relationship can be talked down, given a good reason.

Potions & Scrolls

Potions of Healing and common remedies are sold by any merchant. Greater potions and specialty supplies come from apothecaries licensed by the Snowfall Guild, and poisons need guild membership, a magistrate’s permit, or a warrant from an Arbiter-sanctioned investigator. Scrolls of 4th level and higher are sold only to buyers the Violet Spires will vouch for.

Enchanted Arms

+1 arms and armor are sold only by smiths licensed by the Mastersmiths’ Conclave and their authorized merchants. Anything stronger comes from certified Conclave workshops, and only to buyers with noble lineage, a High Forum recommendation, exceptional service to the crown, or a proven military need. Foci and other magic items are sold by dealers licensed by the Violet Spires.
